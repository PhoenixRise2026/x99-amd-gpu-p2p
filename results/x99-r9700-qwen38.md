# SGLang TP=2 A/B Evidence: Qwen3.8-27B-AWQ

## Scope

This is the archived stock-RCCL/SHM versus patched-RCCL/direct-P2P comparison on one Broadwell-EP host with two AMD Radeon AI PRO R9700 GPUs. It is a case study, not a general performance guarantee. The A/B changes the RCCL configuration/library and selected transport together; the results associate the observed mode with these measurements but do not isolate P2P as the only possible cause of every difference.

Raw per-run values and logs are in [`real_llm_tp2_stock_vs_p2p_ab_results.json`](../examples/broadwell-r9700/real_llm_tp2_stock_vs_p2p_ab_results.json). The stock logs record `isAllDirectP2p 0` and `via SHM/direct/direct`; the patched run records `isAllDirectP2p 1` and `via P2P/IPC`. Hardware, software versions, and source hashes are listed in [the evidence manifest](evidence-manifest.json).

## Prefill

Five runs per mode are recorded at each tier. Values are median prefill throughput; parenthesized ranges are the observed min–max across those five runs.

| Prompt tier (actual tokens) | Stock SHM tok/s | Direct P2P tok/s | Median change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1K (1,052) | 1,147.34 (1,146.55–1,151.44) | 1,515.28 (1,493.73–1,515.56) | +32.1% |
| 4K (4,130) | 14,630.07 (14,162.83–14,989.80) | 14,092.97 (13,152.14–14,752.94) | −3.7% |
| 16K (16,386) | 47,797.23 (46,046.88–48,436.72) | 48,035.57 (47,455.46–49,746.72) | +0.5% |
| 32K (32,745) | 14,947.56 (14,679.38–15,030.80) | 18,764.07 (18,589.61–18,799.73) | +25.5% |

The 4K median is lower in the direct-P2P configuration. The min–max ranges overlap at 4K and 16K, while the 1K and 32K ranges do not overlap. The five-run, four-tier sample is mixed and small; it should not be summarized as a consistent prefill improvement.

## Decode

Single-request decode used 193 prompt tokens and 512 generated tokens. Five runs per mode:

| Metric | Stock SHM | Direct P2P | Median change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Decode throughput | 30.93 (30.92–30.98) tok/s | 31.28 (31.16–31.34) tok/s | +1.13% |
| TPOT | 32.33 ms | 31.97 ms | −0.36 ms |
| TTFT | 262.70 ms | 261.45 ms | −1.25 ms |
| End-to-end latency | 16.78 s | 16.60 s | −0.18 s |

Concurrent decode used 369 prompt tokens and 256 generated tokens per request. Each reported aggregate is the median of three batches:

| Concurrency | Stock SHM aggregate tok/s | Direct P2P aggregate tok/s | Median change |
| ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| C=1 | 31.01 (30.99–31.04) | 31.31 (31.25–31.44) | +1.0% |
| C=2 | 37.61 (37.56–37.67) | 37.90 (37.81–37.93) | +0.8% |
| C=4 | 69.41 (69.39–69.47) | 71.10 (71.10–71.24) | +2.4% |

These modest decode differences are observations from the recorded setup. They do not establish that all of the difference came from the transport change.

## Keep the layers separate

- TransferBench measures selected GPU copy paths.
- RCCL AllReduce reports collective algorithm/bus bandwidth.
- SGLang measures application-level prefill and decode throughput.

The lower-level results show successful peer transfers and RCCL transport selection in this run. The end-to-end measurements show small decode changes and mixed prefill changes. Do not infer a universal LLM speedup from the P2P copy or AllReduce bandwidth.

Other inference changes (for example FP8, MTP, or DFlash2) are separate experiments. No attribution to P2P is made without an A/B on the same software stack and workload.
